Disruptopia — Quick Reference

A ruthless engine builder. 2–5 players, 1–2 hours.

Goal

The game ends after the round in which any player reaches Model Version 7. The player with the most Victory Points wins. Score from:

  • 1 VP per 5 Power
  • 1 VP per Model Version
  • 1 VP per Region with Presence
  • Net Worth bonuses from claimed Millionaire / Billionaire VP Tokens (0–2 VP each)
  • Personal Funds race — 2p: 3 VP for 1st. 3p: 3/1. 4–5p: 3/2/1.

Each Round

1. All players draw 1 card from each deck (Research / Influence / Sabotage), discard down to hand limit 5, and secretly place numbered Tech Workers on Quarterly Strategy actions.
2. Starting from the Player 1 Token and going clockwise, each player resolves actions in Tech Worker order. Pay costs from Corporate Funds, not Personal Funds.
3. Pass the Player 1 Token clockwise; begin the next round.

The 8 Actions

Buy Chips — raise Compute Level (Startup ≤2, Millionaire ≤4, Billionaire ≤7).
Recruit — take the lowest-numbered worker; usable this round. Net Worth gates W4 / W5–6 / W7–8.
Train Model — needs Compute > Model. Costs 1W (M1–2), 2W (M3–4), 3W (M5), 4W (M6–7). +1 Rep and +1 Power per 2 Regions of Presence.
Increase Net Worth — Startup→Millionaire: $3, −2 Rep. Millionaire→Billionaire: $5, −4 Rep. Claim highest available VP Token at that tier.
Marketing — Startup: +3 Rep. Millionaire: +1 Rep, +1 Power. Billionaire: +2 Power.
Scale Presence — pay for next presence token, place in a region adjacent to your existing presence. Claim 1 Subsidy Token there if any remain.
Play Card — place workers equal to the card's cost (0–2) on Play Card. Must meet all requirements when played.
Raise Funds — siphon all Corporate Funds to Personal Funds, then collect Income into Corporate Funds.

Income

Income = Power + (Subsidy Tokens × Net Worth). Startup subsidies are worth $0; Millionaire $1; Billionaire $2. Place 1, 2, or 3 consecutive workers on Raise Funds; the player mat lists the income for that many workers. Fewer workers than your Income cap means you collect the smaller cap, not your full income.

Cards

3 decks: Research (compute / model buffs), Influence (power, reputation, money), Sabotage (attack opponents — sometimes helps you too).

Each card is either an Action Card (play then discard) or an Effect Card (after play, parks in an Effect Slot; you may replay it once per round by spending its worker cost again). Hand limit is 5 at the end of each draw phase.

Reputation Bonus Tiles

Highest-Reputation players claim Tiles by tier: Tile 1 (any Net Worth), Tile 2 (Millionaire+, Rep ≥6), Tile 3 (Billionaire, Rep ≥10). Once Tile 2/3 is claimed it stays even if Rep drops. If Rep ever falls to −3 you are forced to take a negative Tile 0 until you climb back to 0. In a 4–5p game the top two Rep holders each claim a Tile per tier.

Hard Limits

Power: 1 — 30
Income: $0 — $39
Reputation: −3 — 10
Hand limit: 5
Tiebreak (after VP): highest Model Version → most Power → most Personal Funds → highest Reputation → shared failure to become a sociopathically dominant monopoly.
